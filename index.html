Hi! I am a third-year PhD student at the University of Maryland, CLIP Lab, advised by Mohit Iyyer, with a research focus on natural language processing. My work lies at the intersection of evaluation alignment, with the goal of making large language models better able to understand, reason over, and reliably answer questions in long-context scenarios. I initially started my PhD at UMass NLP and later transferred to UMD along with my advisor.

Before starting my PhD, I worked at Hyundai Motors Group and LG Electronics as a research engineer. I was selected as a specialist in AI and conducted research at CMU LTI as a visiting scientist mentored by Jaime Carbonell.

Research

Broadly, I study two related levels of this problem:

  • Context level: How can we help models better retrieve and synthesize relevant information from long contexts?
  • Generation level: How can we rigorously evaluate the quality and faithfulness of long-form generation and reasoning?

My current research includes:

  • Evaluating faithfulness and factuality in long-context and long-form generation [ FABLES (COLM’24), VERISCORE (EMNLP’24), OneRULER (COLM’25) ]
  • Improving LLMs using synthetic datasets for efficient training and stronger reasoning [ BLEUBERI (NeurIPS’25), ongoing work ]
  • Retrieving and organizing information with function-calling agents

Overall, I am interested in building models that are more logical, more faithful, and more dependable in complex long-context settings.
