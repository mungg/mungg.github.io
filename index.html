Research

My work lies at the intersection of evaluation and alignment, with the goal of making large language models better able to understand, reason over, and reliably answer questions in long-context scenarios.

Broadly, I study two related levels of this problem:

  • Input level (context understanding & retrieval) — How can we help models better retrieve and synthesize relevant information from long contexts?
  • Output level (generation quality & faithfulness) — How can we rigorously evaluate the quality and faithfulness of long-form generation and reasoning?

My current research interests include:

  • Evaluating faithfulness and factuality in long-context and long-form generation [ FABLES (COLM’24) , VERISCORE (EMNLP’24) , OneRULER (COLM’25) ]
  • Improving LLMs using synthetic datasets for efficient training and stronger reasoning in long-context scenarios [ BLEUBERI (NeurIPS’25) , ongoing work ]
  • Retrieving and organizing information with function-calling agents in long-context settings

Overall, I am interested in building models that are more logical, more faithful, and more dependable in complex long-context settings.
