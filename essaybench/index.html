EssayBench

work in progress

Non-fiction argumentative-essay extraction pipeline — structured outline, evidence inventory, and epistemic-stance annotation. Adapted from StoryScope/NarraBench for argumentative writing. Working case study: Boston Review Forum "How Not to Defeat Authoritarianism" (Bonica & Grumbach, Winter 2026).

① Schema & extraction demo

Single-pass Stage-1 prompt + dry-run result on the lead essay (thesis, stance structure, claims, evidence, pivots, epistemics, style). Grounded in argumentation-theory literature (Toulmin · Stab & Gurevych · Walton · RST · ICLE++ · Hyland).

② Essay landscape in embedding space

Text-embedding PCA of 39 essays (17 human + 11 Claude + 11 Codex) responding to the same forum. Interactive scatter plot with per-essay stance on hover; human vs LLM evidence comparison tables, overlap matrix, per-essay breakdown.
